Welcome to Marybeth D'Amico's website!

It is with deep regret that we inform you that Marybeth, a remarkable artist, mother and friend passed away in September of 2015. May her music forever be in the hearts of her family, friends and loyal fans.

The good news is that she left us a final album "Great and Solemn Wild", produced by her longtime friend, Pat Byrne. The album and a special vinyl edition are available now!

Friends and fans of Marybeth gathered on November 30th, 2017 to celebrate the release of Great & Solemn Wild. Some video and photos are available from this night.

You can find all her music on iTunes, Spotify, CDBaby and other digital outlets.

Great & Solemn Wild is also available at the following US stores:

Waterloo Records, Austin TX – Reckless Records, Chicago IL
Academy Records, Brooklyn NY – Princeton Record Exchange, NJ
Jackpot Records, Portland, OR – Angry Mom Records, Ithaca NY
and Iris Records, Jersey City NJ!
